Admission Enquiry

Shmt Neera Gupta

Director

It is said that when you have one child you become a parent, when you have two kids you become a referee but when you have multiple kids you become an expert. We, the management and faculty of Gyan Deep Sr. Sec. School are proud to exhibit our expertise with each child that has become a part of our family.
True to our motto, “future begins here”, we believe that the fate of our future i.e. our children, lies in the hands of the educators. They mould the thoughts and configure the minds of generations to come. Focusing on this, we empower the intellect of students with new methods and objective psychology following the modern education system. Our emphasis on “learning by doing” prepares them to put forward novel ideas and to face different situations confidently. Our extracurricular activities like sports, music, dance, art & craft, languages, cultural events, impart a diverse blend of knowledge to students.

We at Gyan Deep Group, ensure a three-way interaction between students, teachers and parents to create and maintain a congenial and cohesive atmosphere, helping our children to flourish in areas of their aptitude and interest. Hence, full cooperation of our diligent parents is of utmost importance, in order to provide a loving, caring, inspiring and self-disciplined atmosphere for the all-round growth of the children.
Dear students, school is a phase of life, where you are full of energy and enthusiasm, having joys, fears while striving hard to achieve. The decisions you make, the friends you choose, the responsibilities you accept shape your life to be stimulating and purposeful. Always remember that learning is not limited to the school, nor it will conclude with passing out classes. It’s a lifelong process. By embracing school’s vision, I wish you to be

  • – open and adaptive to changes
  • – honest and integrated
  • – honest and integrated
  • – A leader imbibed with compassion and empathy
  • – persuasive but humble
  • – honest and integrated

I also implore all our students to rise with greater zeal after every fall. Challenge yourself beyond your comfort zone and think critically but creatively. Make your mark and contribute positively to the society. Be an overcomer.
We, at Gyan Deep Group, continuously work to fulfil this by laying emphasis on high morals and character building which is the key to a bright future. Before concluding, I sincerely appreciate our dedicated staff for all their efforts and cooperation.
“The big secret in life is that there is no big secret. Whatever your goal, you can get there if you are willing to work”- Oprah Winfrey.
