TRANSPORTATION

Our school ensures safe bus transport across Gurugram with cameras, staff, andFirst-Aid.

SPORTS

Our school integrates sports and fitness with facilities, competitions, and holistic student growth.

SECURITY

Our school ensures strict security with parent ID cards, 24/7 trained staff, and advanced CCTV surveillance.

AWARDS

Our school honors excellence with scholar, merit, participation, and certificates annually.

REMEDIAL CLASSES

Remedial classes support students during school hours and holidays to match peers.

LIBRARY

Our library nurtures reading habits with vast books, journals, and knowledge.

SCHOOL COUNSELOR

We supports students with guidance, care, and emotional assistance.

SAFETY

Safe water, reliable electricity, natural light, fresh air, and green surroundings ensure student comfort.

The auditorium hosts events, competitions, and performances with modern audio-visual facilities.

MUSIC STUDIO

Music training enhances learning, reduces stress, and fosters appreciation for all forms.

DANCE STUDIO

Dance training helps students express themselves and discover their interests effectively.

DRAMA ROOM

Theatre enhances communication, creativity, academics, and provides a powerful medium for self-expression.

The Hindi Club fosters language skills, creativity, and interest in speaking and writing.

MATHS LAB

The Maths lab simplifies complex concepts using well-equipped facilities for better understanding.

COMPUTER LABS

Well-equipped computer labs in junior and senior wings promote awareness and versatility of computers.

PERSONALITY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME

Personality development begins early, nurtured through values and an environment that fosters uniqueness.

PARENTS, STUDENT, TEACHER RELATIONSHIP

All departments have smart classrooms with advanced technology to enhance teaching and learning.
